## What is Solarecliente?

Solarecliente is a modern client management frontend application designed to streamline customer data management and interactions. Built with a focus on usability and performance, it provides teams with the tools they need to maintain strong client relationships.

## Who is Solarecliente for?

Solarecliente is designed for teams and organizations that need to:

- **Manage client relationships** - Track interactions, notes, and important client information
- **Visualize data** - Understand trends and patterns in your client base
- **Collaborate effectively** - Share client information securely across your team
- **Scale operations** - Handle growing client databases without sacrificing performance
